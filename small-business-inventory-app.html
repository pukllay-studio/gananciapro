Inventory and sales tracker for small business

GananciaPro keeps your stock and your money in the same place. Each sale lowers the stock and adds the income, so you always know what is left and what you earned.

Try it free for 14 days No card needed. Cancel any time.

What it tracks

  • Products with category, colour, code and a reference photo.
  • Stock by size: each size is a variant of the same product.
  • Stock in, sales, losses, damage, internal use and adjustments, with stock before and after.
  • Income and expenses in your currency, with expense categories and recurring monthly costs.
  • A dashboard with profit, margin, best sellers and low stock.

How a sale works

Pick the product, enter the quantity and the unit price. The total is worked out for you. When you save, stock goes down and the income shows up in your finances.

Who it is for

Clothing and shoe shops, small stores, catalogue sellers and anyone selling through WhatsApp or social media. It runs in the browser on phone, tablet and computer, in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Chinese.

Yes. Each entry is saved with the currency you choose.

Yes. You can download a full copy or export your entries to CSV at any time.
